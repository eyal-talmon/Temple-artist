Help raise the Temple.

The Temple is built by volunteers and funded by the people who hold it dear. Every gift, large or small, goes into the structure and the crew who raise it.

Your donation becomes timber and hardware, tools and safety gear, transport to Black Rock City, and the meals that keep a build crew going through long days on the playa.

At the end of the week the Temple is released to the fire, carrying everything left inside it. Thank you for helping it stand, even for a little while.
